# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standard

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, as its code of conduct. The full text is available at:

- https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
- Chinese translation: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at shareur@imyang.me. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, reject contributions, or block accounts that violate this code. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant Enforcement Guidelines.
